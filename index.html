Logging

The gym tracker that keeps up

Dial the plates, log the set, beat last time. Replex remembers what you lifted and gets the next set ready before you are.

  • The plate loader shows exactly what goes on each side of the bar.
  • Every set starts from what you did last time, so you just confirm or add weight.
  • “This would be a PR” lights up before you lift, not after.
  • The rest timer starts on its own — a set time or until your heart rate recovers — and follows you to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Apple Watch

Log sets from your Apple Watch

Leave the phone in your bag. Turn the crown to set weight and reps, tap LOG, and your iPhone stays in sync in real time.

  • Weight and reps on the crown, with a detent for every plate.
  • A rest countdown on your wrist, with a tap when it's time to lift.
  • Heart rate, zones and calories while you train, saved to Apple Health.
  • Start on either device. The other one picks it up.

Progress

Watch every rep become progress

Volume that climbs week after week, every record you set, and the trend of every lift — so you know the work is paying off.

  • Weekly volume, sessions and your weekly goal at a glance.
  • Records for every exercise, like your heaviest weight and most reps.
  • A chart for each lift, from your first session to today.
  • Weekly and monthly recaps you can share.

AI Coach · Pro

Your AI workout planner

Ask for a week and get it on your calendar. The Coach reads your real training — sessions, records, runs — and plans around it.

  • “Plan next week: 3 strength days and 2 runs” becomes dated sessions, at the times you train.
  • It builds or edits templates from the exercise library and spaces muscle groups for recovery.
  • Ask about any workout or run and get a straight answer from your own numbers.
  • Nothing changes until you confirm it.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Replex works fully on iPhone. With an Apple Watch you can log sets, run the rest timer and track heart rate from your wrist.

Is Replex free?

Yes. Replex is free to use: logging, the rest timer, 5 bodyweight plans, records for your recent sessions and 5 Apple Watch workouts. Replex Pro — monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase — adds the AI Coach, all 44 plans, your own templates and scheduling, full history and unlimited Watch workouts.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your private iCloud. Workouts can also be saved to Apple Health. The AI Coach only sees what it needs to answer you, and only when you ask.

Does it work with pounds?

Yes: kilograms, pounds or stones, and the plate loader follows your unit.

Which languages does Replex support?

54, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic and Hebrew.
