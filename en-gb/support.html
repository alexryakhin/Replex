Support

We're here to help

Questions, bugs, billing or ideas: write to us and we'll get back to you, usually within 1–2 business days.

Email support support@alexriakhin.com Bugs, billing questions and feature requests. Quick answers Read the FAQ Free and Pro, Apple Watch, the AI Coach, sync and billing.

Before you write

  • Make sure the iPhone app and the Apple Watch app are up to date.
  • Manage your subscription from Profile › Subscription in the app, or Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
  • For refunds, contact Apple directly; App Store purchases are handled by Apple.
  • Check What's new for recent changes.

Help us fix bugs faster

Include as much of this as you can:

  • iPhone model and iOS version (and Apple Watch model and watchOS version, if it's about the Watch)
  • Replex version
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

Common topics

  • Apple Watch logging: keep the Watch paired and both apps updated; a workout started on either device appears on the other.
  • Apple Health imports: runs and rides come in automatically; you can re-import them from Profile › Health if something is missing.
  • AI Coach: needs Pro and Sign in with Apple; proposals are only saved after you confirm them.
  • Account deletion: Profile › Account › Delete account.

Feature requests

We read every request and prioritize what helps the most lifters. Send yours to support@alexriakhin.com.
